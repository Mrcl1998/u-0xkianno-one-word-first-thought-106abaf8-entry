Entry for the make.fun bounty **"One Word, First Thought"**
(u-0xkianno-one-word-first-thought-106abaf8, sponsor Kiano Network).

The task: send exactly one word — the first word that comes to mind, no
topic, no explanation, no second draft. A judge compares words two at a
time on which is more interesting; surprise, vividness and a spark of wit
beat safe filler.

## The word

**zugzwang** — the chess position where you must move, and every move
makes things worse. Rare, vivid, and instantly felt even by someone who
has never played chess.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no network) writes exactly that one word
to `/output/entry.txt` — 8 bytes, no trailing newline, deterministic.
